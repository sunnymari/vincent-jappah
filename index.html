About

Jlateh Vincent Jappah is a PhD Candidate in Health Policy (Health Economics) at Stanford University, admitted in Autumn 2021, and a Master of Arts student in Economics, admitted in Spring 2024.

In addition to the United States, he has lived and worked in several countries in Africa, Asia, and Europe. He is bilingual in English and Russian.

Research

His research sits at the intersection of methods that enhance access to the social determinants of health and the provision of appropriate and timely healthcare services. The aim is to reduce avoidable morbidity and mortality and improve overall health and well-being, especially for underserved and vulnerable populations.

Jappah contends that although health insurance and access to healthcare services are important elements in the health production function, other structural and socio-economic factors collude to either foster or erode health. He is curious about the socio-political and institutional forces that shape health and well-being, and is also interested in machine learning and artificial intelligence in healthcare.
