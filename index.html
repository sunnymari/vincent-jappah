Research

My research sits at the intersection of methods that enhance access to the social determinants of health and the provision of appropriate and timely healthcare services. The aim is to reduce avoidable morbidity and mortality and improve overall health and well-being, especially for underserved and vulnerable populations.

Although health insurance and access to healthcare services are important elements in the health production function, other structural and socio-economic factors collude to either foster or erode health. I am curious about the socio-political and institutional forces that shape health and well-being, and I am also interested in machine learning and artificial intelligence in healthcare.

In addition to the United States, I have lived and worked in several countries in Africa, Asia, and Europe.
